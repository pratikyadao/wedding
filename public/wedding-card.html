Wedding Dress Code

Suggested attire palette & vibes for our celebration ceremonies

CARNIVAL & FLOWER HALDI

Festive, Pastel & Floral Prints

Light, breezy, and colorful attire perfect for a playful afternoon of games, smiles, and fresh flower petals.

Ladies: Floral lehengas, pastel anarkalis, or bright georgette sarees.
Gentlemen: Vibrant yellow / pastel kurtas, printed Nehru jackets, or linen shirts.
SANGEET NIGHT

Indo-Western & Evening Glamour

Glamorous, stylish, and dance-floor ready for an unforgettable night of high-energy beats and joyful performances.

Ladies: Shimmery lehengas, sequins, contemporary gowns, or cocktail drape sarees.
Gentlemen: Bandhgalas, Indo-western blazers, dark tailored suits, or stylish sherwanis.
WEDDING CEREMONY

Traditional Maharashtrian / Indian Attire

Graceful traditional heritage wear honoring auspicious morning rituals and timeless cultural elegance.

Ladies: Classic Paithani silk, Nauvari, Kanjeevaram sarees, or royal bridal silks.
Gentlemen: Traditional Kurta-Dhoti / Churidar with Pheta & Uparna, or silk kurta sets.
RECEPTION

Royal Formal & Evening Glamour

Sophisticated evening wear to raise a toast, celebrate, and dine in majestic celebration with the newlyweds.

Ladies: Designer evening gowns, rich zari silk sarees, or elegant reception lehengas.
Gentlemen: Classic black-tie tuxedos, sharp formal suits, or royal achkans.
